## 1. Overview

This article describes Meridian Robotics' code of conduct: core principles, prohibited
conduct, and the process for reporting and investigating a violation.

## 2. Core Principles

All employees are expected to act with honesty, respect, and fairness toward
colleagues, customers, and partners, and to avoid conflicts of interest between
personal financial interests and company decisions.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

Prohibited conduct includes: harassment or discrimination of any kind, falsifying
expense reports or timesheets, sharing confidential company or customer data outside
authorized channels, and accepting gifts from vendors valued over $100 without
disclosure to the Ethics desk.

## 4. Reporting a Violation

Violations may be reported through the confidential Ethics Hotline (accessible from the
HR portal footer) or directly to an HR business partner. Reports may be filed
anonymously.

## 5. Investigation Process

All reports are triaged by the Ethics & Compliance team within 3 business days. A
formal investigation, if opened, is typically completed within 30 days and involves
interviews with relevant parties and a review of documentary evidence.

## 6. Disciplinary Actions

| Severity | Example | Typical Action |
|---|---|---|
| Minor | Late expense report submission | Verbal warning |
| Moderate | Unauthorized data sharing | Written warning, mandatory retraining |
| Severe | Harassment, discrimination, fraud | Suspension or termination |

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

- **Can I report anonymously?** Yes, via the Ethics Hotline.
- **Will I face retaliation for reporting?** No; retaliation against a good-faith
  reporter is itself a violation of this policy and is subject to disciplinary action.
- **What counts as a "gift" that needs disclosure?** Anything of value over $100,
  including meals, event tickets, and physical items.

## 8. Escalation

If an employee believes their report was not adequately investigated, they may escalate
directly to the Chief People Officer.
