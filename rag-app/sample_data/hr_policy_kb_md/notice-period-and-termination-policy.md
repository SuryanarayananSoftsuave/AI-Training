## 1. Overview

This article describes required notice periods for resignation, the involuntary
termination process, and final settlement timelines. Notice-period requirements are
jurisdiction-specific and vary by the employee's country of employment.

## 2. Notice Period by Jurisdiction

Meridian Robotics currently operates employment contracts in three jurisdictions. This
table is the authoritative reference for notice-period length; do not assume a figure
from one jurisdiction applies to another.

| Jurisdiction | Notice Period (Employee Resignation) | Notice Period (Company-Initiated) |
|---|---|---|
| India | 60 days | 60 days |
| United States | 14 days | 0 days (at-will, subject to local exceptions) |
| United Kingdom | 30 days | 30 days, or longer if specified in the contract |

**This policy currently covers only India, the United States, and the United Kingdom.**
Employees in any other jurisdiction should contact their local HR business partner
directly, since no standard notice period is defined here for jurisdictions outside
this table.

## 3. Resignation Process

A resignation is submitted in writing via the HR portal's "Separation > Resign" flow.
The notice period begins on the date the resignation is formally acknowledged by the
manager in the portal, not the date it was verbally communicated.

## 4. Involuntary Termination

Involuntary terminations require sign-off from HR and Legal prior to the employee being
notified, following the disciplinary escalation path described in the Code of Conduct
Policy where applicable.

## 5. Final Settlement

Final settlement (last salary, unused earned-leave payout per the Employee Leave
Policy, and any pending expense reimbursements) is processed within 45 days of the last
working day for India and the UK, and within the next standard payroll cycle for the
US.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

- **Can I negotiate a shorter notice period?** Yes, subject to mutual written agreement
  between the employee and manager, recorded in the HR portal.
- **Is unused earned leave paid out on exit?** Yes, per the carry-over and payout rules
  in the Employee Leave Policy (2024 Edition).
- **What if my contract specifies a different notice period than this table?** The
  individual employment contract takes precedence over this policy where the two
  conflict.

## 7. Escalation

Any dispute about notice-period length or final settlement amount is escalated to the
HR Shared Services desk, with Legal consulted for jurisdiction-specific questions.
