## 1. Overview

This article describes health insurance coverage, enrollment windows, and how to file a
claim.

## 2. Health Insurance Coverage

| Plan Tier | Monthly Premium (Company-Paid) | Employee Contribution | Coverage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employee Only | $450 | $0 | Individual medical, dental, vision |
| Employee + Spouse | $780 | $120/month | Above + spouse |
| Employee + Family | $1,050 | $210/month | Above + dependent children |

## 3. Enrollment Windows

New employees may enroll within **30 days** of their start date; coverage begins on the
1st of the following month. Outside of new-hire enrollment, changes are only permitted
during the annual **Open Enrollment period** (November 1-15) or after a qualifying life
event (marriage, birth/adoption, loss of other coverage), reported within 30 days of
the event.

## 4. Dependents

Eligible dependents are a legal spouse and children under 26 years of age. Documentation
(marriage certificate, birth certificate) must be uploaded to the HR portal within 30
days of adding a dependent.

## 5. Claims Process

Most claims are processed directly between the provider and the insurance carrier.
Employees needing reimbursement for an out-of-network claim submit the provider's
itemized statement through the Benefits tab of the HR portal; reimbursement is
typically issued within 15 business days.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

- **Can I add a dependent outside Open Enrollment?** Only after a qualifying life
  event, reported within 30 days.
- **What happens if I miss the 30-day new-hire enrollment window?** Coverage cannot
  begin until the next Open Enrollment period, absent a qualifying life event.
- **Is dental included by default?** Yes, dental and vision are bundled into every
  tier.

## 7. Escalation

Disputes over a denied claim should first be raised with the insurance carrier directly;
if unresolved after 30 days, escalate to the Benefits team via the HR portal.
