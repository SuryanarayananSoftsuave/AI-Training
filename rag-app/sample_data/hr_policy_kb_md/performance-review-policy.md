## 1. Overview

This article describes the performance review cycle, rating scale, and how reviews
connect to compensation decisions.

## 2. Review Cycle & Timeline

Formal performance reviews run twice a year: a mid-year check-in (June) and an
annual review (December), each preceded by a 2-week self-assessment window. Employees
who joined less than 90 days before a review date are excluded from that cycle and
reviewed at the next one instead.

## 3. Rating Scale

| Rating | Label | Typical Distribution |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | ~5% of employees |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | ~20% of employees |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | ~55% of employees |
| 2 | Needs Improvement | ~15% of employees |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | ~5% of employees |

## 4. Goal Setting

Employees set 3-5 goals at the start of each cycle in the Performance tab of the HR
portal, reviewed and agreed with their manager within the first 2 weeks. Goals should
follow the SMART framework (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).

## 5. Promotion & Compensation Review Link

Compensation adjustments are considered only at the annual (December) review, using the
rating from that cycle. A rating of 4 or 5 in two consecutive annual cycles is a
prerequisite for promotion consideration, though it does not guarantee promotion, which
also depends on role availability.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

- **Does extended leave affect my review timing?** Yes — see the Employee Leave Policy;
  an employee on leave covering more than half of a review period is moved to the next
  cycle.
- **Can a rating be appealed?** Yes, within 10 business days of the rating being shared,
  via the Performance tab.
- **Are mid-year ratings used for compensation?** No, only the annual (December) rating
  feeds compensation decisions.

## 7. Escalation

Rating disputes not resolved by the direct manager within 10 business days are escalated
to the department head and an HR business partner jointly.
