## 1. Overview

This article describes Meridian Robotics' employee leave policy as it applies to all
full-time employees, effective **2024-01-01**. It covers leave entitlements, the
request process, and carry-over rules. This edition supersedes the 2023 edition of this
policy; HR business partners should use the entitlement numbers in this article for any
leave taken on or after 2024-01-01.

## 2. Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for leave from their date of joining. Interns and
contractors are not covered by this policy and should refer to their engagement
agreement instead. (Unchanged from the 2023 edition.)

## 3. Leave Entitlements

Leave is split across five categories. Casual, sick, and earned leave entitlements
increased from the 2023 edition; a new Bereavement Leave category was also added.

### 3.1 Casual Leave

The annual entitlement is **15 days** (increased from 12 days in 2023). Casual leave
must still be requested at least 3 business days in advance through the HR portal.

### 3.2 Sick Leave

The annual entitlement is **12 days** (increased from 10 days in 2023). Sick leave may
be reported on the day of absence; a doctor's note is required for absences longer than
2 consecutive days.

### 3.3 Earned Leave

Earned leave accrues at a rate of 1.75 days per completed month of service, for an
annual entitlement of **21 days** (increased from 18 days in 2023). Earned leave
requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance.

### 3.4 Parental Leave

Parental leave remains **26 weeks** for the primary caregiver and **2 weeks** for the
secondary caregiver (unchanged from 2023).

### 3.5 Bereavement Leave (New)

Employees are entitled to **5 days** of paid bereavement leave for the death of an
immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, or sibling), and **2 days** for an
extended family member. This category did not exist under the 2023 edition.

## 4. Requesting Leave

Unchanged from the 2023 edition: all leave requests (except same-day sick leave) are
submitted through the HR portal under "Time Off > New Request," and auto-escalate to
the manager's manager after 3 business days if unconfirmed.

## 5. Carry-Over Rules

Unused earned leave carries over to the next calendar year, up to a maximum of **12
days** (increased from 10 days in 2023). Casual, sick, and bereavement leave do not
carry over and expire at the end of the calendar year.

## 6. What Changed from the 2023 Policy

| Leave Type | 2023 Entitlement | 2024 Entitlement |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave | 12 days | 15 days |
| Sick Leave | 10 days | 12 days |
| Earned Leave | 18 days | 21 days |
| Parental Leave | 26 weeks (primary) | 26 weeks (primary), unchanged |
| Bereavement Leave | Not offered | 5 days |
| Earned Leave Carry-Over Cap | 10 days | 12 days |

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

- **I was told casual leave is 12 days — is that right?** No, that was the 2023
  entitlement. As of 2024-01-01 casual leave is 15 days.
- **Can I take casual leave and sick leave in the same week?** Yes, they are tracked
  independently.
- **Is bereavement leave paid?** Yes, at full base salary.

## 8. Escalation

Disputes about leave balances should be escalated to the HR Shared Services desk, which
will review the employee's accrual history against the entitlements in this edition and
issue a correction within 5 business days if warranted.
