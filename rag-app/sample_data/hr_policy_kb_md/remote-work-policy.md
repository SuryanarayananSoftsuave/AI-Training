## 1. Overview

This article describes Meridian Robotics' remote work policy: who is eligible, how many
days per week can be worked remotely, and the approval process.

## 2. Eligibility

Employees in engineering, product, design, and support roles may work remotely, subject
to manager approval. Roles requiring physical presence (manufacturing floor, hardware
lab, on-site reception) are not eligible for this policy and should refer to their
department's own attendance guidelines.

## 3. Remote Work Days & Core Hours

Eligible employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. Remote work requests
are submitted via the same HR portal used for leave requests, under "Time Off > Remote
Work Request." Employees working remotely must remain reachable during core hours,
**10:00 AM to 4:00 PM** in their local time zone.

## 4. Equipment & Expenses

Meridian Robotics provides a one-time **$500 home office stipend** for eligible
employees' first fully-remote quarter, covering desks, chairs, or monitors. Ongoing
internet costs are not reimbursed. See the Expense Reimbursement Policy for the general
claims process this stipend follows.

## 5. Approval Process

A remote work request is approved by the direct manager within the HR portal. Requests
spanning more than 4 consecutive weeks additionally require sign-off from the
department head.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

- **Can I work remotely 5 days a week permanently?** Not under this policy; that
  requires a separate fully-remote role designation approved by HR leadership, outside
  the scope of this article.
- **Do remote days count against my leave balance?** No, remote work and leave are
  tracked independently.
- **What time zone do core hours use?** The employee's own local time zone, not
  headquarters time.

## 7. Escalation

If a manager repeatedly denies remote work requests without stated reason, employees
may escalate to their HR business partner for a policy-compliance review.
