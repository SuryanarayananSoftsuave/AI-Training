## 1. Overview

This article describes Meridian Robotics' employee leave policy as it applies to all
full-time employees. It covers leave entitlements, the request process, and carry-over
rules. HR business partners should reference this article when answering employee
questions about how much leave they have and how to request it.

**Note:** This version of the policy was effective from 2023-01-01. It has been
superseded by the 2024 edition (see Cross-Article References below). It is retained
here for historical reference and for processing leave requests dated before
2024-01-01.

## 2. Eligibility

All full-time employees are eligible for leave from their date of joining. Interns and
contractors are not covered by this policy and should refer to their engagement
agreement instead.

## 3. Leave Entitlements

Leave is split across four categories, each with its own annual entitlement.

### 3.1 Casual Leave

Casual leave covers short, planned absences (personal errands, family events). The
annual entitlement is **12 days**. Casual leave must be requested at least 3 business
days in advance through the HR portal.

### 3.2 Sick Leave

Sick leave covers illness or medical appointments. The annual entitlement is **10
days**. Unlike casual leave, sick leave may be reported on the day of absence rather
than in advance; a doctor's note is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive
days.

### 3.3 Earned Leave

Earned leave accrues at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service, for an
annual entitlement of **18 days**. Earned leave requests must be submitted at least 5
business days in advance.

### 3.4 Parental Leave

Parental leave is granted at **26 weeks** for the primary caregiver and **2 weeks** for
the secondary caregiver, regardless of tenure.

## 4. Requesting Leave

All leave requests (except same-day sick leave) are submitted through the HR portal
under "Time Off > New Request." A request is considered approved once the reporting
manager confirms it in the portal; unconfirmed requests auto-escalate to the manager's
manager after 3 business days.

## 5. Carry-Over Rules

Unused earned leave carries over to the next calendar year, up to a maximum of **10
days**. Casual leave and sick leave do not carry over and expire at the end of the
calendar year.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

- **Can I take casual leave and sick leave in the same week?** Yes, they are tracked
  independently.
- **What happens if I don't use my earned leave?** Up to 10 days carry over; anything
  beyond that is forfeited on December 31.
- **Is parental leave paid?** Yes, at full base salary for the duration listed above.

## 7. Escalation

Disputes about leave balances should be escalated to the HR Shared Services desk, which
will review the employee's accrual history and issue a correction within 5 business
days if warranted.
