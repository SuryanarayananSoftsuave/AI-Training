## 1. Overview

This article describes how employees claim reimbursement for pre-approved business
expenses, including travel, client entertainment, and the remote-work home office
stipend.

## 2. Eligible Expenses

Eligible categories are: travel (flights, trains, mileage), lodging, client meals and
entertainment, professional development (courses, conference tickets), and the one-time
home office stipend described in the Remote Work Policy. Personal expenses, commuting
costs, and alcohol at client meals are not eligible.

## 3. Submission Process & Deadlines

Claims are submitted through the Expenses tab of the HR portal, referencing the
internal cost center code and with original receipts attached. **Claims must be filed
within 30 days of the expense date**; claims filed later are rejected automatically by
the system and require a manual exception request to Finance.

## 4. Approval Thresholds

| Claim Amount | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Up to $200 | Auto-approved, manager notified |
| $200.01 - $500 | Direct manager approval |
| $500.01 - $2,000 | Direct manager + department head approval |
| Above $2,000 | Department head + Finance approval |

## 5. Travel Expense Rules

Domestic flights must be booked in economy class unless the flight duration exceeds 6
hours, in which case premium economy is permitted. Mileage for personal vehicle use is
reimbursed at the standard rate published quarterly on the Finance intranet page.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

- **Can I expense a client dinner including alcohol?** No, alcohol is excluded from
  reimbursable client entertainment expenses.
- **What if I lose a receipt?** A signed statement explaining the expense may be
  submitted instead for claims under $50; above that, Finance may reject the claim.
- **How long does reimbursement take once approved?** Approved claims are paid out in
  the next payroll cycle, typically within 10 business days.

## 7. Escalation

Claims rejected for missing the 30-day deadline may be escalated to Finance via a
manual exception request, which requires a written justification from the employee's
manager.
